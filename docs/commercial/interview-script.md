# Customer discovery script

Internal guide for an explicitly authorized interview. Do not contact prospects
or record/share their information without the necessary authorization. Use opaque
case references in notes; do not collect customer identities or raw transfer PII.

## 30-minute discovery

1. **Last real incident (8 minutes).** Walk through the last difficult transfer
   investigation or evidence request. What triggered it? Which people and systems
   were involved? What was missing? How long did active work and waiting take?
   Ask for an authorized synthetic reconstruction, not sensitive records in chat.
2. **Frequency and cost (5 minutes).** How often does this happen? What source
   measures that frequency? How many touches and handoffs occur? What was delayed?
   Separate measured values, estimates and unknowns.
3. **Alternatives (4 minutes).** What do current vendors/logs provide? What would
   a small internal script solve? What failed in previous integration attempts?
   What would make adding another system unacceptable?
4. **Authority and workflow (5 minutes).** Who owns policy, approves source trust,
   accepts residual disagreement and reviews exports? Where would findings need
   to appear? Who can supply approved examples and engineering time?
5. **Buying process (5 minutes).** Which budget funds this problem? Who signs?
   What procurement/security steps and competing priorities exist? What would
   constitute a worthwhile result? When would a decision realistically happen?
6. **Next step (3 minutes).** Summarize the observed problem and uncertainty. Ask
   for one bounded next action: a synthetic case walkthrough, baseline measurement,
   technical review, or a budget-owner meeting. Record the owner and date.

Do not lead with ZK, a product tour or a question asking whether the prospect
"likes the idea." Ask how they would solve the job without Clearproof. Only after
understanding the job, demonstrate the relevant existing workflow and its limits.

## Short prototype review

Have the operator perform an investigation, compare policy outcomes, and ask a
reviewer to inspect a portable record. Observe where they get stuck without
coaching. Ask which output they would use again and what they still cannot trust.
Show a missing-evidence and disagreement case as well as a successful case.
Explain that cryptographic validity, policy outcome, exchange and settlement are
separate results, and that local keys/counterparties are developmental/synthetic.

## Notes template

- Prospect alias and role; interview authorization; date and note owner.
- Workflow/job and last incident reference.
- Current process, alternatives, measured frequency and effort; source for each.
- Pain/consequence in the prospect's own words, paraphrased with permission.
- Missing evidence, operational owner, integration and procurement constraints.
- Budget authority: identified / not identified; no inferred purchasing intent.
- Next action, owner, date and explicit commitment (or none).
- Disconfirming evidence and reason to stop or change the hypothesis.

Interest, compliments and hypothetical price acceptance are weak evidence. A
provided case, scheduled technical review, allocated engineer, procurement action
or signed engagement is stronger, but none alone proves sustained adoption.

## Planned pilot work

1. Correct storage parsing, chain-cache isolation and discovery compatibility.
2. Establish authenticated proof inputs, transfer binding, durable tenant state and verifier consistency.
3. Add policy-change simulation with explained outcomes.
4. Add read-only transfer investigations across compliance, counterparty and settlement events.
5. Add independently verifiable historical evidence exports.
6. Add observation-mode onboarding, simulated counterparties and a reproducible pilot.

These are planned milestones, not generally available features. External credential-wallet integrations and ongoing provider-driven re-screening depend on an identified partner and supported upstream access.

The proposed commercial direction is managed integrations and operational workflows around portable evidence. No customer adoption, pricing, service-level commitment or regulatory certification is asserted here.
